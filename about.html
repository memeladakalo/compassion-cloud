About Compassion Cloud

Technology that keeps people and relief efforts connected.

We provide a centralized digital space for coordinating information, requests, resources and people during disaster response.

Our purpose

Make coordinated relief easier.

Disaster response can involve many organizations, volunteers, donors and affected communities. Compassion Cloud is designed to reduce information gaps by bringing key response activities into one accessible platform.

The system supports transparent communication, structured relief requests, resource visibility and volunteer participation while keeping the community at the centre of every response.

Our principles

  • Community first
  • Clear and timely information
  • Responsible resource coordination
  • Accessible digital services
  • Respect and dignity
